# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Customers ordering from their phones at a table, kitchen and floor staff working during service, counter staff collecting payment, and the cafe owner reviewing operations and finances across devices.

## Product Purpose

Strike Yard is a QR table-ordering and cafe operations system for a sports venue in Nepal. It should move an order from customer to kitchen to payment with minimal explanation, while giving the owner a trustworthy view of sales, costs, stock, and service quality.

## Brand Personality

Warm, capable, energetic. The product should feel grounded in a real neighborhood cafe and sports venue, not like generic restaurant software.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, excessive cards, glass effects, finance-app formality, cramped enterprise tables, and clever controls that slow down staff during a rush.

## Design Principles

- Make the next operational action obvious.
- Keep money and order status trustworthy and auditable.
- Use familiar controls that work quickly on phones and shared counter screens.
- Show useful decisions, not just raw data.
- Keep customer, kitchen, counter, and owner workflows connected through one source of truth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong contrast, visible focus states, touch targets of at least 44 pixels for primary mobile actions, reduced-motion support, text labels alongside color, and plain language suitable for mixed technical confidence.
